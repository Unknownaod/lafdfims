Fire Code

Fire and life safety codes, regulations, and requirements

Los Angeles Fire Code

The fire code establishes requirements intended to protect life and property from fire and other hazards. Requirements may apply to buildings, businesses, construction projects, special events, fire protection systems, and other activities.

The information provided through FIMS is intended to help users locate relevant fire prevention and fire safety resources.

California Fire Code

The California Fire Code contains statewide fire and life safety provisions. Local jurisdictions may adopt amendments and additional requirements applicable to their area.
