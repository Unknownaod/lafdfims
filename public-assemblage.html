Public Assemblage & Event Safety

Public gatherings and special events require careful consideration of fire and life safety. Organizers are responsible for planning safe access, occupant movement, emergency exits, and appropriate fire protection measures.

Depending on the size, location, occupancy, and nature of an event, additional review or approvals may be required.

Public Assemblage Requirements

Occupant Capacity

Event organizers should maintain occupancy levels appropriate for the venue and applicable requirements.

Emergency Exits

Emergency exits and paths of egress must remain accessible and free from obstructions.

Fire Extinguishers

Appropriate fire extinguishers and fire protection equipment may be required based on the event.

Emergency Access

Fire department access routes must remain clear so emergency personnel can reach the event area.

Temporary Structures

Tents, canopies, stages, and other temporary structures may require additional review.

Special Events

Certain public events may require an application or fire safety review before the event takes place.

Event Safety Checklist

  • Maintain clear emergency exits and evacuation routes.
  • Do not block fire lanes or emergency access roads.
  • Keep required fire protection equipment accessible.
  • Maintain safe occupant capacity.
  • Ensure temporary structures are properly installed.
  • Maintain access for emergency personnel.
  • Follow applicable fire and life safety requirements.

Temporary Events

Temporary events may involve additional fire safety considerations because of temporary structures, cooking equipment, generators, decorations, crowd size, or other event-specific hazards.

Event Planning Notice Requirements vary depending on the event and location. Event organizers should submit required applications and allow sufficient time for review before the scheduled event.
